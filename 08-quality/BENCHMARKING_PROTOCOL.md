# Benchmarking Protocol

## Purpose

Convert vague goals such as “commercial quality” or “better than competitors” into explicit, evidence-backed targets.

## Benchmark brief

For each project, record:

- benchmark purpose;
- comparable products/references;
- dimensions being compared;
- expected market baseline;
- target level for VSO's product;
- intentional non-competitive areas;
- desired differentiators;
- evidence date/source;
- assumptions and uncertainty.

## Rules

1. Benchmarking informs requirements; it does not authorize copying protected implementation/content.
2. Fresh public-market facts must be verified at the time the benchmark is created or refreshed.
3. A benchmark must identify what VSO intends to match, exceed, differentiate, or deliberately omit.
4. Benchmark gaps become requirements, risks, accepted trade-offs, or backlog items.
5. Release readiness must disclose unresolved gaps against mandatory commercial targets.
