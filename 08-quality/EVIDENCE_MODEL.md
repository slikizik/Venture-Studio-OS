# Evidence Model

Evidence proves why VSO believes a requirement, criterion, gate, or release state is valid.

Evidence may be a test result, artifact, file, link, screenshot/reference, owner approval, benchmark comparison, review record, or structured note.

## Evidence quality

Each evidence item must identify:

- what claim it supports;
- source/type;
- time produced;
- relevant project/work packet/criterion;
- checksum or stable reference where applicable;
- verification result;
- whether it becomes immutable after approval.

## Principle

A status value is not evidence. “Passed”, “done”, or “commercial” is only a conclusion derived from evidence.
