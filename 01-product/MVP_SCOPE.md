# MVP Scope

## Included

- Single-user local web application
- Project CRUD, archive, restore, search, and filtering
- Project Brain records
- Template-driven stages, deliverables, and quality gates
- Hierarchical deliverables
- Structured work packets
- Review and approval workflow
- Decision and risk logs
- Version records and change history
- Portfolio dashboard and project dashboard
- Seed templates and representative demo projects
- Local persistent database
- Automated tests and release packaging

## Required project templates

Software, Book, Learning Product, Business Venture, Creative Product, and Custom.


## Product-alignment requirements

- Project Intent Brief and explicit success/commercial-quality targets
- Requirement and acceptance-criteria traceability back to intent
- Quality Profile and benchmark brief
- Evidence-backed work/deliverable/release gates
- Learning records feeding controlled future change
- Autonomy and owner-attention diagnostic metrics
- SEVL end-to-end proof by the end of Phase 05

The MVP architecture is frozen after successful Phase 00 under `01-product/ARCHITECTURE_FREEZE.md`. Non-blocking additions go to `01-product/BACKLOG.md`.
