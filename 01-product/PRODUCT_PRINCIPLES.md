# Product Principles

1. Product intent and stored records are authoritative; implementation must serve the intended outcome.
2. Commercial quality and governed autonomy are equally non-negotiable.
3. Every project has intent, blueprint, requirements, deliverables, work packets, quality gates, evidence, and history.
4. Templates configure workflows; project types are not separate hard-coded applications.
5. AI output is never complete without evidence and verification.
6. Humans approve high-consequence decisions; routine execution should not require routine supervision.
7. Every important change is versioned and traceable.
8. “Technically working” and “commercially ready” are distinct states.
9. Feedback and failures must feed a controlled learning loop rather than ad-hoc rework.
10. Owner attention is a scarce resource: VSO should reduce unnecessary escalations without hiding risk.
11. Complexity should be progressively disclosed so non-technical owners can govern work and technical experts can inspect deeply.
12. Before the SEVL is proven, optional infrastructure and polish must not displace completion of the end-to-end product loop.
