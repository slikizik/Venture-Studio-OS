# Success Criteria

## MVP product success

The MVP succeeds when a single owner can manage VSO plus representative Software, Learning Product, and Book projects through the same generic operating model without project-specific application forks.

The first proof milestone is the Smallest Executable VSO Loop defined in `01-product/SEVL_SCOPE.md`.

## Commercial-development success

VSO must distinguish functional completion from commercial readiness through project Quality Profiles, benchmark targets, retained evidence, quality gates, and release-readiness states.

## Autonomy/lifestyle success

VSO must make it possible for approved work to continue without constant owner supervision, while escalating consequential decisions with enough plain-language context for the owner to respond remotely.

Track at minimum:

- autonomy rate;
- owner attention time;
- escalation count and quality;
- first-pass acceptance rate;
- autonomous recovery rate;
- requirement/evidence coverage.

These metrics are diagnostic, not targets that may be gamed by reducing testing or hiding escalations.

## Integrity success

Required workflows persist data, survive restart, retain review/decision/version history, and link completed claims to evidence.
