# Architecture Decisions

## ADR-001 Modular monolith

Chosen to reduce deployment and coordination complexity while preserving clear domain boundaries.

## ADR-002 Template-driven universality

Project types are configuration templates over shared domain entities.

## ADR-003 Local-first MVP

SQLite enables simple installation, persistence, backup, and testing.

## ADR-004 Immutable review history

Review and approval decisions are append-only records.
